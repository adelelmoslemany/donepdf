# ✏️ Edit PDF Online – Change the Text Already in Your PDF

A typo in a contract. A wrong date on an invoice. A name spelled incorrectly in a certificate.

When the original Word file is gone and all you have is the PDF, most free tools only let you **add new text on top** of the page. Changing the words that are already there usually means paying for desktop software.

This guide explains how online PDF editing works, what you can change, and how to edit even **scanned PDFs**.

---

## 🔍 What Can a PDF Editor Change?

A good online PDF editor lets you:

- Edit the **existing text** of a PDF in place
- Turn **scanned pages** into editable text with OCR
- Add new text, images, shapes and comments
- Highlight, underline, strike out or white out content
- Sign documents and fill in PDF forms
- Rotate, reorder, insert or delete pages

It is useful for office workers, students, freelancers, teachers and small businesses.

---

## ❌ Why PDFs Are Hard to Edit

PDF was designed to **look the same everywhere**, not to be edited.

Common problems include:

- Text is stored as positioned pieces, not as paragraphs
- Fonts in the file may not be installed on your computer
- Scanned PDFs contain **pictures of text**, not real text
- Arabic and other right-to-left languages often break when edited: letters come apart, or words appear in reverse order

That is why many "PDF editors" can only place new text over the page.

---

## ✅ How Online PDF Editing Works

A modern browser-based editor solves these problems step by step:

- It reads the text of the page and its exact position, size and colour
- When you click a line, it covers the original and places editable text in the same spot
- For scanned pages, OCR recognizes the text so it can be edited too
- When you download, the changes are written into the PDF as real PDF content

The result looks like the original document, with your corrections in place.

## 🚀 Best Practices

- Replace short pieces of text (a word or a line) for the most natural result
- If the new text looks different, try another font from the list until it matches
- After OCR, read the recognized text carefully, especially numbers and formulas
- Use **Ctrl+Z** (or **Cmd+Z** on a Mac) to undo any change
- Open the downloaded PDF and zoom in on your edits before sending it
- Covering text hides it on the page but does not remove it from the file: for sensitive information, use [Redact PDF](https://donepdf.com/redact-pdf), which is made for removing it
