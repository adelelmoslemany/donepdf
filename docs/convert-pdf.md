## 🔍 What Can You Convert a PDF Into?

PDF conversion tools let you turn a PDF into:

- **Word** documents, to edit text and layout
- **Excel** spreadsheets, to work with tables and numbers
- **PowerPoint** presentations, to reuse slides
- **Images** (JPG, PNG and more), to share pages or use them in designs
- **Plain text, HTML, Markdown or CSV**, to reuse content in websites, apps or data tools
- **PDF/A**, for long-term archiving

And in the other direction, you can turn Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images or web pages **into PDF**.

---

## ❌ Why Converting PDFs Is Tricky

A PDF does not store a document the way Word or Excel does.

Common problems include:

- Text is saved as positioned pieces, so paragraphs and columns must be rebuilt
- Tables are just lines and text, not real cells
- Fonts may be missing or replaced
- Scanned PDFs contain pictures of text, so there is nothing to copy until OCR reads it
- Right-to-left languages like Arabic can come out reversed or broken

That is why two converters can give very different results for the same file.

---

## ✅ How a Good PDF Converter Helps

A reliable converter:

- Rebuilds paragraphs, headings and lists instead of separate text boxes
- Detects tables and turns them into real rows and columns
- Keeps images in place and at good quality
- Uses OCR for scanned pages, so the text becomes editable
- Handles different languages and writing directions correctly

The goal is a file you can **work with right away**, not one you have to fix by hand.
